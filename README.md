# Awesome-Medical-Information-Management

## Top Medical Information Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Medical Inquiry Handling, Content Management, HCP Response, Compliance & Life-Sciences Medical Affairs*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Medical Information (MI) Management**. These systems help pharmaceutical and life-sciences companies receive, document, respond to, and report medical inquiries from healthcare professionals and patients while maintaining regulatory compliance and consistent scientific content.



**Examples** include Veeva MedInquiry, PharmaForce IQ, Ennov Medical Information, True Blue MI, DrugDev, Within3, IQVIA MI, ArisGlobal MI, EXTEDO MI, and MasterControl MI (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Domain-specific Medical Information platforms for regulated pharma are almost entirely commercial. There is no widely adopted open-source equivalent for global MI case handling, approved-content libraries, and health-authority reporting. Open building blocks exist only at the edges (content repositories, ticketing, general health information systems). This section is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[General ticketing and case-management open systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source service-desk and case tools (e.g., Zammad, osTicket, TheHive-style platforms) that can be adapted for inquiry intake—**not** pharma-validated MI systems.



- **[Content and document control open repositories](https://github.com/)**  

  Open document-management and wiki systems used to store draft scientific response documents under internal control.



- **[ERPNext / Odoo healthcare and quality modules](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open ERP modules that handle some healthcare or quality processes; far from a full Medical Information suite.



- **[Open health information and knowledge bases](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects for structuring medical or drug knowledge (useful for internal reference, not regulated MI response).



- **[Pharmacoinformatics and drug-data open repositories](https://github.com/)**  

  Research-oriented open databases and scripts for approved product information—supporting evidence gathering rather than inquiry workflows.



- **[Secure form and intake open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open form builders and encrypted intake channels that can feed a formal MI process.



- **[Audit-trail and logging open components](https://github.com/)**  

  Libraries and systems for immutable logging of actions—useful building blocks for compliance evidence.



- **[Workflow and approval open engines](https://github.com/)**  

  Open workflow tools for routing drafts and approvals of medical responses under human oversight.



- **[Redaction and privacy open utilities](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools that assist with PII/PHI handling when logging or analyzing inquiry data.



- **[Standards and terminology open resources (FHIR, MedDRA helpers)](https://github.com/)**  

  Open implementations and helpers for healthcare standards occasionally used in broader medical-affairs integrations.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using open **case/ticketing systems** only as a front-end intake layer that feeds a validated commercial MI platform.

- Maintaining **controlled content libraries** in open document systems while publishing approved responses exclusively through regulated channels.

- Accepting that global inquiry handling, approved scientific content management, health-authority reporting, and validated audit trails still require commercial MI platforms (Veeva MedInquiry, IQVIA, ArisGlobal, Ennov, PharmaForce, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on internal knowledge organization, non-GxP collaboration, and integration adapters—not as the system of record for medical information responses.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Capture inquiries via secure forms or open ticketing → route to medical reviewers → pull from a controlled content library → respond and log in a validated commercial MI system → retain audit evidence for inspections. Open tools may support non-regulated steps only. Pharmaceutical companies almost universally rely on commercial Medical Information platforms for compliance and consistency.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Medical Information activities in the pharmaceutical industry are highly regulated. Responses to healthcare professionals and patients must follow strict scientific, promotional, and pharmacovigilance rules. Open-source or self-built systems are generally **not** appropriate as the primary MI system of record without extensive validation and regulatory oversight. This list is not legal, regulatory, or medical advice. Incorrect handling of medical inquiries can create significant compliance and patient-safety risk.
